Map key style guide

Fills and outlines in the key have slightly rounded corners. This sets them apart from interactive elements, such as checkboxes, which have square corners.

Area symbols

A simple key with no groups. Each item shows a small symbol next to its label.

Ramp

A ramp shows ordered steps, such as low to high, as one bar split into bands. When every label fits on one line, the bands sit side by side with each label underneath.

In a ramp, only the two outer ends are rounded. This keeps ramps consistent with other symbols in the key, while the bands meet with square edges so they read as one continuous bar.

Ramp with outlines

When bands have an outline, a small gap separates them. Patterns repeat across each band at their normal size.

Stacked ramp

When a label is too long to fit under its band, the ramp stacks instead, with each label beside its band. Rows are spaced the same as in other groups.
